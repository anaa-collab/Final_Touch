# Final Touch Cleaning — SEO / AEO / Local Search Checklist

Goal: show up fast for **"post-construction cleaning [city]"** searches and get
quoted by AI answers (Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity, voice). This is
tuned for a **local service business** — local SEO + AEO is where your leads are,
not generic blog SEO.

---

## 1 · Keyword map (target these exact phrases)

| Intent | Primary phrase | Where it lives |
|---|---|---|
| Money keyword | post-construction cleaning Orlando / Tampa / Central Florida | Homepage title, H1 area, first paragraph |
| Service | rough clean · detail pass · final touch-up · punch-list cleaning | Services section H2/H3 |
| Buyer | post-construction cleaning for builders / general contractors | Who-we-serve + a dedicated page |
| Multi-family | multi-family turnover cleaning Florida | Services + FAQ |
| Trust | licensed insured post-construction cleaning | FAQ + footer |
| Cost | how much does post-construction cleaning cost | FAQ (AEO answer) |

**Don't keyword-stuff.** Each phrase should appear naturally once or twice per
page. Google rewards the *entity* (a real local cleaning business) more than
repetition.

## 2 · Per-page SEO settings (Squarespace: Page → Settings → SEO)

**Homepage**
- SEO Title: `Post-Construction Cleaning in Central Florida | Final Touch Cleaning`
- Description: `Post-construction cleaning & final detail for builders, GCs, and
  property managers in Orlando, Tampa & Central Florida. Licensed & insured,
  documented sign-off, same-week scheduling. Call (321) 233-3513.`

**Service pages** (create one each — great for ranking on specific terms):
- `/post-construction-cleaning` → "Post-Construction Cleaning for Florida Builders | Final Touch"
- `/multi-family-turnover` → "Multi-Family Turnover Cleaning | Orlando & Tampa | Final Touch"
- `/rough-clean` , `/final-detail`

**City pages** (the biggest local-SEO lever): one thin, genuine page per city
you serve — `/orlando`, `/tampa`, `/winter-park`. Same structure, city-specific
H1, intro, and a line about that market. This is how you rank in multiple cities
without one page competing against itself.

> Squarespace tip: keep URL slugs short and keyword-first (`/orlando`, not
> `/service-areas/orlando-fl-cleaning-1`).

## 3 · Structured data (already provided — just keep it accurate)

- **`LocalBusiness` / `HomeAndConstructionBusiness`** → `site-header.html`
  injection. Update `address`, `geo`, `openingHoursSpecification`, and `sameAs`
  (your real Google Maps, Facebook, Instagram URLs).

  indexing in **Search Console**.
- Add **`Review` / `AggregateRating`** schema once you have real reviews (don't
  fake it — it's a manual-action risk).

## 4 · AEO — get cited by AI answers

AI engines quote **clear, self-contained answers**. Your FAQ is the surface.

- Phrase FAQ questions the way people ask them ("how much does post-construction
  cleaning cost?"), and make the **first sentence a complete answer** — AI lifts
  that sentence.
- Add a short **"How it works"** paragraph near the process steps that a machine
  can quote verbatim.
- Keep facts consistent everywhere (hours, service area, phone). AI cross-checks
  your site, Google Business Profile, and directories — mismatches lower trust.
- Publish 2–3 genuinely useful articles that answer buyer questions
  ("What a GC should expect from a post-construction clean," "Rough clean vs.
  final detail — what's the difference?"). These become AEO citation bait.

## 5 · Google Business Profile (highest-ROI local move)

1. Create/claim the profile. Category: **Construction cleaning** (+ secondary
   "Commercial cleaning service").
2. Service area: list Orlando, Tampa, Winter Park + surrounding.
3. Add real photos (same ones as the site), services, hours, phone that **exactly
   match** the site and schema.
4. Get reviews from builders — ask right after a signed-off job. Reviews +
   proximity + relevance = the local pack ranking. This is where most of your
   early leads will come from before organic ranks.

- [ ] Connect **Google Search Console** (Settings → Marketing → SEO) + submit sitemap (`/sitemap.xml`).
- [ ] Connect **Google Analytics 4**.

  ("post-construction-detail-clean-winter-park.jpg", alt "Detailed kitchen after
  post-construction cleaning in Winter Park").

- [ ] Set a social share image (Settings → Marketing → Share Buttons / OG).
- [ ] Enable SSL (default on) and confirm canonical domain (www vs non-www).
